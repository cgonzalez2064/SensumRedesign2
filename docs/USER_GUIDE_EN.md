# User guide — Sensum Construcciones Content Manager

The Content Manager lets you change the text, photos, PDF portfolio and contact
details of **sensumconstrucciones.com** without technical knowledge. Changes go
live on the website as soon as you press **Save changes** or **Publish**.

Panel address: **https://app.sensumconstrucciones.com**
(the public website stays at https://sensumconstrucciones.com)

(The panel opens in Spanish by default; switch to English as described in §2.)

---

## 1. Signing in and out

**Sign in**
1. Open https://app.sensumconstrucciones.com
2. Enter your email and password and press **Sign in**. Tap the eye icon to see
   what you typed.

After several wrong attempts in a row, the panel asks you to wait a few minutes
(protection against intruders).

**Two-step sign-in.** If your account has it on, after the password the panel asks
for the **6-digit code** from your authenticator app (Google Authenticator,
Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password…). Without your phone, tap **Use a recovery
code** and type one of the codes you saved (each works once).

**Sign out**
Open the menu with your name (top right) and choose **Sign out**. Always do this
on a computer that isn't yours.

For security, your session ends after 60 minutes without activity. If that
happens while you're editing, the panel asks for your password right there and
**you won't lose what you were typing**. It also ends if an administrator changes
your e-mail or password; if your e-mail changed, tap **E-mail changed? Use
another** and type the new one.

**First time:** you'll receive an invitation email. Open it, tap **Create my
password**, choose a password, and you're in. The link expires after 72 hours;
if it expired, ask an administrator to send a new one. When your account is
activated you get a **welcome e-mail** with your role, first steps and how to
get help.

**Welcome (first time only):** before you go in, the panel asks you to choose the
**language** (Español or English; it is also the language of the e-mails it sends
you) and the **appearance** (Light, Dark or **Automatic**, which follows your device and
is selected by default). Each option applies at once so you can see it; press **Save
and continue**. You can change both any time in **My account**.

**Home:** greets you with what you can do in your role (for example, Sales and leads
sees "follow up on leads and see the visit statistics") and shows three **Getting
started** steps for your role. Once you tap **Got it** they don't show again, on any
device.

## 2. Language and appearance

In the menu with your name (top right):
* **Language:** Español or English.
* **Appearance:** Light, Dark or System (follows your device).

Your choice is remembered. You can also change it in **My account**.

**The top stays in place.** When you scroll, the top bar (menu, logo, "Request
support" and your name) stays put. On computers the logo and "Content Manager" also stay
at the top of the side menu even when the menu is long; on phones and tablets the same
happens inside the slide-out menu.

## 3. Editing text

1. Go to **Website text**.
2. Choose a section, e.g. *Home → Main section* or *Home → Services*. Each card
   explains which part of the website it contains.
3. Every text has two versions: **Spanish** (the main one) and **English**
   (shown when a visitor chooses "EN" on the website).
4. Type the new text. Below it you'll see how many characters you've used and the
   **recommended length**: what looks best in the design, on phones and computers. If
   you need more, keep typing: paragraphs take twice the recommendation, titles half as
   much again and labels and buttons a little more (the Google title and description
   keep their limit: Google cuts longer ones). Past the recommendation the counter
   turns orange and reminds you to check how it looks on the site, especially on phones.
5. Press **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom. You'll see "Changes published
   on the website" with a link to view it.

**Automatic translation** (if it's on, you'll see a blue notice when you open a
section; it works the same in the **contact-form options** and the **photo
descriptions**):
* When you type in **Spanish**, the **English** box fills in by itself a second
  after you stop typing (and the other way round). Below it you'll see
  "Translated automatically from Spanish. Please review.": read it and fix it if
  needed before saving.
* **Undo** brings back the previous text and keeps it while you go on editing
  the other language.
* If you correct the translated text yourself, the panel won't change it again:
  you can write each language separately and both are kept.
* **Translate from Spanish** / **Translate from English** translates that text
  again whenever you want.
* If a translation is longer than the maximum, the field turns red: shorten it
  before saving.
* If translation isn't available at that moment, the panel tells you and
  changes nothing; just type the text yourself.

Tips:
* **Highlight a word in color** (main title only): wrap it in asterisks, e.g.
  `Built with *precision*`. A preview shows the result.
* **Restore original text** brings back the approved website text.
* The `<` and `>` signs can't be used.
* If you try to leave with unsaved changes, the panel warns you.
* If someone else saved the same section while you were editing, the panel warns
  you so nobody's work is lost.

### Title and description in Google

The **Google (search results)** section holds the title and description that Google and
link previews (WhatsApp, Facebook) show for the site. **They are not visible on the
page.**

* **Title:** up to 60 characters, with the words people search for first
  (e.g. "Constructora en Guatemala…").
* **Description:** 120 to 160 characters; summarize the services and invite a quote.
* Google may take days to weeks to show a change.
* More details in `docs/GUIA_SEO.md` (Spanish).

## 4. Contact details

Under **Contact details** you change phones, WhatsApp number, email, address,
Google Maps link and Instagram. They update everywhere on the website: contact
section, footer, green WhatsApp button, error page and privacy notice.

* **Mobile phone** is optional: leave it empty to show only the office number.
* **WhatsApp:** include the country code, e.g. `+502 3481 9804`.
* **Address:** the panel previews how it will look in each place.
* **Google Maps:** search the address in Google Maps, tap **Share** and copy the
  link. Use **Test link** to check it.

### Contact form: questions, order and who receives the messages

Under **Contact form** (*Website* menu) you decide what the website asks visitors:

* **Change a question:** tap the pencil ✎. Write the question in Spanish (English is
  optional: if empty, English shows the Spanish), an optional **help text** (small
  print under it), whether it is **Required**, and whether it takes a **half row** or
  a **full row** (two half-row questions in a row sit side by side on computers).
  Press **Done**.
* **Add a question:** choose the kind of answer: **Short text**, **Paragraph**,
  **E-mail**, **Phone**, **Number** (with minimum and maximum), **Date** (can ask for
  dates from today on only), **Drop-down list**, **Single choice** (all options
  visible) or **Checkboxes** (several can be ticked). Lists, single choices and
  checkboxes have their **options** in Spanish and English.
* **Reorder:** drag by the ⋮⋮ handle or use the ↑ ↓ arrows.
* **Remove:** the bin icon; **Undo** appears for a few seconds.
* **Fixed rules:** the **name** is always asked and required. **E-mail** and **phone**
  can be removed one at a time, but at least one stays and is required, so the
  customer can be answered.
* The **"Service of interest"** options are edited the same way (pencil → Options).
* The **Preview** works like the website's form (nothing is sent): switch between
  **Phone**, **Tablet** and **Computer** and between Spanish and English; unsaved
  changes say **New** or **Edited**. Type and press **Send message** to see the error
  messages visitors would see.
* **Save and publish** updates the website right away. **Restore the original form**
  brings back the 5 approved questions (then press Save).
* **Who receives the messages?** (owner account only): up to 3 addresses. Saving asks
  for your password and sends you a security notice. Everyone else only sees them.

The answers arrive in each message's e-mail, are kept in **Prospectos** ("Other
answers from the form") and appear in the CSV. Up to 15 questions and 20 options per
question. Ask only what you need — short forms get more messages — and never ask for
sensitive data (health, ID documents, bank details).

## 5. Changing photos

Go to **Photos & files**. There are three tabs: **About**, **Types of projects**
and **Documents**. Each slot explains where the photo appears on the website.

**Upload or change a photo**
1. Tap **Upload photo** or **Change image** (in galleries: **Add photo**).
2. Choose the image (on a phone you can use your gallery or camera).
3. Check the **preview**: it shows how the photo will be cropped on the website.
   If the photo isn't the right shape, choose the **Framing** (e.g. *Top*,
   *Center*, *Bottom*) to decide which part is kept.
4. Write a short **description** of what's in the photo (e.g. "Remodeled kitchen
   with wooden cabinets"). It helps people with visual impairments and search engines.
5. Press **Publish**. You'll see the upload progress.

**Other actions:** *Edit description*, *Remove photo* (the original illustration
comes back), and in galleries the arrow buttons to change the order.

**Photo requirements**
* Allowed formats: **JPG, PNG and WebP**. Maximum size: **10 MB**.
* **Projects** (card and gallery): landscape, **4:3** shape, recommended
  **1600 × 1200 pixels** or larger (minimum 1000 × 750).
* **About:** square or portrait, recommended **1600 × 1600 pixels** or larger
  (minimum 1000 × 1000), with the important part in the center — it is cropped
  taller on phones and wider on computers.
* Use real, sharp, well-lit photos that the company has permission to publish.
* No need to edit them first: the system crops, resizes and optimizes them. Very
  large photos are reduced before uploading.

**PDF portfolio** (*Documents* tab): **Replace PDF** uploads the portfolio that
the website's button downloads. Maximum 20 MB. You can go back to the sample
document.

## 6. Your account and password

In **My account**:
* **Profile:** your name, panel language and appearance.
* **Account / Security — change password:** enter your current password, the new
  one, and confirm it. For security you'll be signed out on your other devices and
  receive a notification email. Use at least 10 characters; a phrase of several
  words is easy to remember and hard to guess. Don't reuse passwords from other sites.
* **Sign out on other devices:** useful if you used someone else's computer.
* **Your role:** your role's name and the sections it includes.
* **More sections / Ask for access:** the menu only shows what you can open. To see
  everything the panel has, tap **More sections** (at the end of the menu, on Home or in
  My account → **See all sections**). There you see **You can ask for** (each section with
  what it includes and an **Ask for access** button) and **You already have access**.
  **Ask for access** opens the request with that section ticked; you can tick others and
  say what for. We e-mail whoever manages users and you get an e-mail when they review
  it; meanwhile the section shows **Requested**. If you open a section your role doesn't
  include, the panel says so and offers **Ask for access** or **See all sections**.
  "User management" and "Support request management" aren't asked for: the owner gives them.
* **Temporary password:** if an administrator gave you a password to sign in, it
  expires after 72 hours. Use it soon: the panel then asks you to create your own.

**Forgot your password?**
1. On the sign-in screen tap **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter your email. You'll receive "Reset your password" (check your spam folder).
3. Open the link and choose a new password. The link expires after 60 minutes and
   works only once.

**Two-step sign-in (recommended; required for the owner account):**
1. In **My account → Two-step sign-in** tap **Turn on** and enter your password.
2. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app (or type the key by hand).
3. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows.
4. Keep the **10 recovery codes** somewhere safe (a password manager or printed).
   You can generate new ones at any time.

**If you change phones:** in **My account → Two-step sign-in** tap **Remove registered
devices** and enter your password. Your authenticator app and remembered browsers are
removed and all your sessions end. When you sign in again:
1. Enter your e-mail and password.
2. **Check your e-mail:** we send a 6-digit code (it expires in 10 minutes). If it
   doesn't arrive, check your spam folder or tap **Resend code** (up to three times).
3. **Register your new device:** scan the QR code with the app on the new phone, enter
   the code and keep the new recovery codes.

If you lost your phone and have no recovery codes, ask whoever manages users (or
technical support) to **remove your registered devices**: you'll sign in with the same
e-mail code step.

**Remember this device:** if the owner account allows it, when you type the code you
can tick "Remember this device for 30 days" and that browser won't ask for it during
that time. Don't tick it on a shared computer. **My account** shows how many browsers
you remembered, and **Forget them** clears them.

Nobody from the technical team will ever ask for your password or your codes, by
email or phone.

## 7. Requesting support (a problem, an improvement or a change)

If something doesn't work, looks wrong, or you have an idea, tap **Request support**
(at the top of every panel screen; on phones it's the life-buoy icon) or **New
request** in **Support**. **New support request** opens:

1. Choose the **Type**: Problem, Improvement / suggestion, Content, Design / visual or Other.
2. Write a short **Title** (e.g. "The Projects image doesn't load").
3. In **Description**, briefly tell us what happened or what you'd like improved.
4. Say **where you noticed it** (website, panel or both). The **Page** is filled
   in with the screen you were on; change or clear it if it happened elsewhere.
5. Optionally attach a **screenshot** (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB).
6. Tap **Send request**.

The request goes straight to the technical support team (it@gruposensum.com).

**Follow-up:** the **Support** menu opens your requests. Under **My requests**
each request has its own page with its status (New, In progress, Waiting for
information, Resolved, Closed) and the conversation with support. You get an e-mail when support
replies, asks for more information or resolves it, and the menu shows a number next
to "Support" when there's a new reply. To answer, write in **Your reply** and tap **Send
reply**: if you were asked for information, or it was marked resolved but the
problem persists, your reply moves it back to "In progress". Closed requests take
no more replies (you can create a new one).

**Rate the help:** when support marks your request as resolved, its page shows **How
did we do?**: choose 1 to 5 stars and, if you like, leave a comment. Without a reply,
the request closes on its own after 7 days.

**Technical information sent automatically:** browser and version, operating
system, device type, window and screen size, panel language and theme, time zone,
connection status, the panel screen you were on, the system version, date and
time, and your name and email. You can see it before sending under "See the
technical information that is sent". Passwords, cookies and anything typed in
other forms are **never** sent.

> **Don't include passwords or sensitive information in the request.**

If the email couldn't be sent at that moment, the panel tells you: your request
**is saved** and you can retry right away or later from **Support**,
where you can also see your request history.

## 8. If a change doesn't appear on the website

1. Reload the website page bypassing the browser cache: **Ctrl + F5** (Windows)
   or **Cmd + Shift + R** (Mac). On a phone, close and reopen the tab.
2. If you're viewing the website in English, remember English text is edited separately.
3. Make sure you saw "Changes published on the website".
4. If it still doesn't show after a few minutes, use **Request support**.

## 9. If an error appears

* Read the message: it almost always explains what happened and what to do
  (e.g. check your internet connection or fix a field marked in red).
* **What you were editing is not lost** — it stays on screen so you can try again.
* If the error repeats, use **Request support** and include the **code** shown in
  the message (e.g. `A1B2C3`); it helps the technical team find the cause.
* If you can't sign in to the panel, email it@gruposensum.com.

## 10. Users and roles

**Users** is seen by the owner account (it@gruposensum.com) and by anyone with
**User management** (part of the "Administrator" role). From there you invite, change,
disable or delete accounts.
* **Invite user:** name, email, **role** and email language. The person receives a link
  to create their password. There are four roles:
  * **Administrator:** the whole panel: text, photos, contact details and form,
    publishing and activity, **Leads**, **Statistics**, **Site health**, **Monthly report
    (view)** and **User management** (does not manage support, send the report or change
    technical settings). Only the owner gives it.
  * **Website editor:** text, photos, PDF portfolio, contact details and form.
  * **Sales and leads:** Leads and full Statistics; does not change the website.
  * **View only:** Statistics (basic version, no personal data), Site health and the
    Monthly report; changes nothing.
* **Extra sections (optional):** under the roles, for an exception: add a section the
  role doesn't include (e.g. "Website editor + Leads"). They are also added when you
  grant a request.
* **Search and review:** above the list type a name, e-mail or role (accents and
  capitals don't matter) or filter by **role**. The tabs **All · Active ·
  Invitations · Turned off** show each group and, under **Review**, appear only when
  someone matches: *Must create password*, *Without two-step sign-in*, *No sign-in in
  90 days* and *Temporary access*. On phones they are in the **Show** menu. **Clear
  filters** shows everyone.
* **On Home**, the **Access** card sums up what needs attention (requests, invitations
  expiring or expired, pending passwords, temporary access ending this week, accounts
  without two-step sign-in or unused); each line opens its filter.
* **Access requests:** when someone asks for more access, it shows above the list with
  what they ask for and their message. **Grant access** applies it in one click and
  **Dismiss** closes it; either way we tell them by e-mail. If they ask for something
  you can't give, only the owner account can.
* **Temporary access** (agencies, photographers or temporary staff): when inviting or
  in **Change role**, turn on **Temporary access** and choose the date. The
  account works until the end of that day; 3 days before we tell the person and whoever
  invited them, and that day it turns off by itself. To extend it, change the date; to
  turn it back on, **Reactivate**.
* When inviting or changing an e-mail, if the domain looks mistyped (e.g. "gmial.com"),
  the panel suggests the right one with **Use this**.
* Each person's "⋮" menu:
  * **Update information.** Corrects their name or e-mail. A new e-mail asks for your password; their sessions end
    and we tell both the old and the new address. If they haven't activated their
    account yet, the invitation goes to the new address.
  * **Send a new-password link.** They get an e-mail to create a new password
    (valid 24 hours); until they use it, their current password keeps working.
  * **Set password.** Useful when e-mails don't reach them. The panel generates an
    easy-to-read one (e.g. "Lago-Pino-Ruta-472") or you type it. It asks for your
    password, ends their sessions and tells them by e-mail **without the password**:
    share it yourself securely. With the box ticked (recommended), the panel asks them
    to create their own password before anything else, and the temporary one **expires
    after 72 hours** if unused. An invited account becomes active. After saving you see
    a **message ready to send** (panel address, e-mail and password, in their language):
    **Copy message** and paste it into WhatsApp.
  * **Two-step sign-in.** Require it for that person (they set it up when they sign
    in) or, if they lost or changed their phone, **Remove registered devices** (asks for
    your password): their app and remembered browsers are removed and their sessions
    end. When they sign in, after their password we send a **code to their e-mail**;
    once they confirm it, they **register their new device**. The list shows "Will
    register a new device" until they do.
  * **Close their sessions.** If they lost their phone or computer: closes the panel on
    all their devices without changing their password. We tell them by e-mail.
  * **See activity.** Opens in **Activity** what they did and what was changed in their account.
  * **Resend invitation**, **Change role** (applies right away),
    **Disable / Re-enable** (signs them out) and **Delete user**.
* **Automatic notices:** when an invitation is about to expire, the person gets a
  reminder with a new link (once); if it expires unaccepted, or when it's accepted, we
  tell whoever invited them.
* Someone who manages users without being the owner only changes people with the
  **same or less access** who don't manage users, and only gives access they have.
  Only the owner gives "User management" and "Support request management". Everyone
  else shows a lock.
* Every change is recorded in the panel's activity. Everyone only sees in the menu what
  they can change; **My account** shows "Your role", and **More sections** everything else.
* Nobody changes their own account from Users (that's **My account**), and the owner
  account can't be changed or deleted from the panel.

## 11. For the owner account (technical support): Monitoring

Only the **it@gruposensum.com** account sees **Monitoring** in the menu
(Administration → Monitoring). There you'll find:

* **Status and figures:** whether the system is working, critical errors in the
  last 24 hours, errors this week, and when the scheduled check last ran.
* **Trends (interactive charts):** the site's response time per day (red dots mark
  days with failed checks), errors per day by severity, and successful and failed
  sign-ins. Choose 7, 30 or 90 days; hover, tap or use the arrow keys to read each
  day; tap a legend name to hide or show that series; "Show data" shows the table.
* **Availability (uptime):** the share of scheduled checks without failures over the
  last 24 hours, 7, 30 and 90 days (green ≥ 99.9 %, yellow ≥ 99 %, red below). Under
  Trends, **Daily availability** shows each day as a coloured bar.
* **Incidents (30 days):** each period when the check found failures: when it started,
  how long it lasted, how many checks failed and **what failed** (for example the
  database or storage). "Ongoing" means it is still happening.
* **Site health (summary):** the button at the top shows what people with the "Site
  health" access see.
* **Error log:** every server, panel and website error, newest first. Filter by
  severity (Critical, Error, Warning), source and period, or search by text or
  by the **reference code** a user saw. "Technical details" shows the code
  location, the request and the user.
* **E-mail alerts:** critical errors are e-mailed automatically to
  it@gruposensum.com (the same error at most once an hour). Use **Send a test
  e-mail** to check they arrive.
* **Activity and errors:** sign-ins, failed attempts, changes, photos, support requests and
  failed e-mails over 24 hours, 7 and 30 days.
* **System:** version, disk space, database size, last backup, and whether the
  published pages match the saved content.

If you receive an alert: open Monitoring, search for the reference code from the
e-mail and look at the technical details. If it was a one-off, nothing else is needed.

## 12. Monthly report for clients

In **Monthly report** you prepare a short, positive report for your clients every
month: the **website's results**, the **work done** and the **monitoring and protection**
that keep it online. It shows the value of the support service.

The preview has two tabs:

* **For clients** — what is sent (e-mail and PDF). Summary, key figures
  (**Availability**, **Visits**, **Potential clients**, **Automatic checks**,
  **Published changes**, **Threats stopped**, **Requests resolved**), **Highlights of
  the month**, **Visits and potential clients**, **Work done this month**,
  **Monitoring and protection** and **Next steps**. Comparisons with the previous month
  (▲ in green) appear only when they improved. It does not mention backups,
  certificates, errors or other technical topics.
* **Technical detail** (internal, never sent) — for the owner account and the team:
  **Security** ("Protected" or "Needs attention" with the exact reason: no HTTPS, no
  recent backups or a certificate about to expire), interruptions with date and time,
  the month's errors, backups, certificate and domain, Google scores, the full service
  level, automatic maintenance and **technical to-dos**.

**Owner account (sending):**

1. **Recipients:** add the e-mails (up to 20) and press **Save recipients**. Your
   account is always copied. Choose the language (Spanish or English).
2. Check the month's **Preview** (you can pick another month).
3. Optionally write a **message for your clients**; it appears at the top.
4. **Send me a test** sends it only to you; **Send the {month} report** sends it to
   everyone (always the **For clients** version, with the PDF attached).
5. **History** shows what was sent.

If last month's report isn't sent in the **first 5 days of the month**, you get a
**reminder** email (once) and a notice on **Home**.

**Administrators (view):** anyone with the **Monthly report (view)** access (part of
the "Administrator" and "View only" roles) finds it under **Site status → Monthly
report** and sees both tabs, without recipients or send buttons.

## 13. For the owner account: e-mail and the form's captcha

In **Administration → E-mail** you decide who sends and who receives e-mail:

* **Website contact form:** which addresses receive visitors' messages (up to 3),
  which address appears as the sender and how they are sent. Replying to a
  message goes straight to the visitor.
* **Panel's sending account:** the address that sends invitations, passwords,
  support requests, the monthly report and alerts, and where replies go. The
  **Fill in the Namecheap details** button fills the server, port and security.
  The mailbox password is stored encrypted and never shown again (leave it empty
  to keep it).
* **Where notices go:** support requests and automatic alerts.
* **Spam protection (captcha):** the built-in captcha is always on and invisible to
  visitors. Optionally turn on **Cloudflare Turnstile** by pasting its Site Key and
  Secret Key (the steps are on the same page).

**Save settings** asks for your password and e-mails you a security notice. Then use
**Send a test e-mail** and **Send a test message** to confirm everything arrives
(check the spam folder too).

## 14. For the owner account: automatic alerts

**Monitoring → Automatic alerts** lists the notices the system sends on its own
(critical errors, changes to the site files, suspicious sign-in attempts, sign-ins
from new devices, HTTPS certificate, domain expiry, e-mail setup (SPF/DKIM/DMARC),
backups, off-server backup, disk space, weekly summary, monthly security review and
the report reminder), when each is sent and when it was last
sent. Tick or untick each box to turn it on or off; it is saved at once.

**Site files → Check now** compares the published files with the installed version.
If you get a change alert and **don't** recognise the changes, don't accept them:
tell technical support. If you made them, press **Mark as expected** (asks for your
password).

**E-mail and domain → Check now** confirms that the domain's e-mail is set up
correctly (SPF, DKIM, DMARC and MX: without them the site's e-mails land in spam or
can be forged) and shows when the domain expires. It is also checked once a day.

## 15. Request management (owner account and technical support)

Under **Support** the owner account —and anyone with the **Support
request management** access (an extra section the owner gives)— sees **everyone's** requests
in **Request management**:

* **Support overview** (at the top, can be folded away): choose 30, 90 or 365 days and
  see the **open**, **received** and **resolved** requests (with the average time to
  resolve), the average first answer, satisfaction, the **Received and resolved per
  day** chart, and bars **by status, type, priority and where it was noticed**.

* **Filters** by status (Open, New, In progress, Waiting for information,
  Resolved, Closed, All) with their counts, and **search** by title, text, person
  or number (`#12`). Unread tickets are bold and listed first; the menu shows how
  many need attention.
* On each ticket: the description, the **screenshot**, the technical information
  and the **conversation**.
* **Actions:** *Mark in progress*, *Ask for more information* (write what you
  need; the person gets an e-mail and the ticket stays "Waiting for
  information"), *Mark as resolved* (with a message about what was done),
  *Close* and *Reopen*. For each you choose whether to notify by e-mail.
* **Reply** to the person (with or without e-mail) or write an **Internal note**
  only technical support sees.
* **Priority** (low, normal, high, urgent) and **Edit** the title, description,
  type and where it was noticed. Everything is recorded in the conversation.
* When the person replies, you get an e-mail and the ticket goes back to "In progress".

The **monthly report** includes a summary of the month's tickets (received,
resolved, average time, pending) and of the automatic maintenance (checks,
security scans, backups, certificate, Google, blocked spam, notices).

**Service level:** the Support overview shows the period's average **first answer**,
the **% within target** and the average **satisfaction** from ratings. The **Response
target** (4, 8, 24, 48 or 72 hours) is chosen right there. Internal notes don't count
as an answer. A 1- or 2-star rating marks the request as unread.

## 16. For the owner account: Security

In **Administration → Security**:

* **Encrypted off-server backup.** The system e-mails an encrypted ZIP with the
  database and the photos as often as you choose (daily, weekly or monthly). Use an
  address that is **not** on this hosting and a backup password of at least 12
  characters (keep it: without it the file can't be opened). **Send backup now**
  is for testing and **Download full backup** saves it on your computer. To open
  it: 7-Zip (Windows) or Keka / The Unarchiver (Mac), with the backup password.
* **Accounts:**
  * **Turn off unused accounts** (Never, or after 90, 120 or 180 days without signing
    in): we warn the person 7 days before; if they don't sign in, the account turns off
    and we tell whoever manages them. The owner account is never turned off.
  * **Refuse leaked passwords** (on): when a password is created or changed, the panel
    checks anonymously that it doesn't appear in known data breaches (Have I Been
    Pwned). The password never leaves the server and, if the service doesn't answer,
    nobody is blocked.
  * **Allow "Remember this device"** (off): people who use two-step sign-in can skip
    the code for 30 days in a trusted browser. Turning it off forgets every remembered browser.
* **Two-step sign-in.** Choose who must use it (only the owner account; the owner and
  whoever administers —manages users or publishes the site—; the whole team; or nobody)
  and see who already has it on. If someone lost or changed their phone, press **Remove
  devices** next to their name: at their next sign-in they confirm a code sent to their
  e-mail and register their new device. If the panel's e-mail isn't working, technical
  support can turn it off from the server with `php ~/sensum-cms/bin/console 2fa:reset <e-mail> --off`.

Every change asks for your password and is logged.

## 17. Leads: the contact-form messages

Visible to the owner account and to people with the **Leads and statistics** access
(for example the "Sales" type).

* Every message from the site's form is kept here, **even if the e-mail notice fails**
  (it then shows a yellow warning). The menu shows how many nobody has opened yet.
* **Filters** by status (Active, New, Contacted, Quoted, Won, Lost, All), by
  **service**, and **search** by name, e-mail, phone, text or number (`#12`).
* On each message: buttons to **reply by e-mail**, **message on WhatsApp** or
  **call**; the **status** and the quoted or won **amount** (quetzales, no decimals);
  and follow-up **notes** ("Called on Tuesday…"). Every change is kept in the history
  with who and when.
* **Owner:** each message can have a person who follows it up (**Owner** field in the
  detail). If you choose someone else, we tell them by e-mail. In the list, filter by
  **Assigned to me** or **Unassigned**.
* **Automation** (below the list): choose the owner of each service and of **Everything
  else**; each new message is assigned to them and arrives by e-mail (the form e-mail
  keeps arriving as always). **Reminder if still "New"** (24, 48 or 72 hours) sends one
  e-mail to the owner, and the **Weekly summary on Mondays** counts new leads and the
  ones waiting for a reply (only when there is something to report).
* **Confirmation to whoever writes** (off until Sensum approves the text): the client
  gets an e-mail as soon as they send the form, in Spanish or English depending on the
  page. In the text you can use {nombre}, {servicio}, {telefono}, {whatsapp} and
  {correo}; **See it with an example** shows the result.
* **Export CSV** downloads the filtered list for Excel or Google Sheets.
* **Delete** removes the message and its notes; use it when the person asks us to
  delete their data.
* The owner account chooses in **Data retention** how long messages are kept (6, 12,
  24 or 36 months); after that they are deleted automatically.

## 18. Visit statistics

* **Visits**, **clicks to get in touch** (WhatsApp, phone and e-mail), **contact-form
  messages** and **contact rate**, compared with the previous period.
* With the **Visit statistics** access but not **Leads** (for example the "View only"
  role) you see the **basic version**: visits, page views, visits from phones,
  where visitors come from, devices and clicks — no contact-form messages or personal
  data.
* **Visits per day** (chart), **where visitors come from** (Google, Facebook, direct…),
  **devices** and language, **actions** on the site (map, portfolio, social media) and
  the path **from message to project** (contacted, quoted, won).
* **Period:** last 7, 30 or 90 days, **this month**, a full month or **Choose dates…**
  (from–to, up to a year). Below it, the period it is compared with. Under "From message

* **Export:** **Spreadsheet (CSV)** with every figure for the period (for Excel or Google
  Sheets), or **Print or save as PDF** (the screen, ready to print).
* **Send by e-mail:** a Sensum-branded report to up to 5 addresses, with the parts you
  choose (visits per day, where they come from, actions, devices and, if you see Leads,

  has totals: never names, e-mails or messages from the people who wrote.
* Measured on the site itself, **without cookies** and without storing IP addresses;
  people asking "Do Not Track" and robots are not counted, so figures are somewhat
  lower than other tools show. The owner account can turn them off.

## 19. Site health

For people with the **Site health** access (part of the "Administrator" and "View only"
roles, or added as an extra section). In the menu: **Site
status → Site health**.

* A notice at the top says whether **everything works**, whether there **was a
  problem** in the last 24 hours, or whether **there is a problem now** (technical
  support is already notified).
* **Availability (uptime)** over the last 24 hours, 7, 30 and 90 days; average

## 20. For the owner account: automatic translation

In **Administration → Translation** you paste the free **DeepL API** key (new keys
include 1,000,000 characters in total; the whole site is about 5,000), switch it on or
off and press **Test connection** to check it works and see how much is used. The
site's ES/EN toggle never uses DeepL: if the plan runs out, English is written by hand
and the site stays the same. When the key reaches **90%** of its characters (and if they
run out) an e-mail goes to the alert address so it can be changed in time. The key is stored encrypted and never
shown again; changes ask for your password. With translation on, typing in one
language fills the other in Website text, contact-form options and photo descriptions.

## 21. For the owner account: "View as" (checking access)

In **Users → View as…** (or a person's "⋮" menu → *View the panel as this person*) you
choose an account and, after confirming your password, see the panel **exactly as that
person sees it**: their menu, screens and data.

* A blue bar at the top shows *Viewing the panel as …*, their role, "read-only"
  and the time left (30 minutes).
* **Switch person** moves to another account without asking for the password again.
* **Back to my account** ends it and takes you back to Users.
* It is **read-only**: trying to save shows that changes are not saved. Nothing is
  marked as read in that person's account either.
* Every start, switch and end is logged in the activity.

## 22. Activity: who changed what

Visible to the owner account, whoever manages users and whoever has "Publishing and
activity". It shows every change in the panel —who made it, to whom and when—,
grouped by day.

* Filter by **Person**, **Kind** (text and publishing, photos and files, users and
  access, leads; the owner also sees sign-ins, security, settings and support) and
  **dates**. **Load more** brings earlier entries.
* **Export to Excel** downloads what the filters show (up to 5,000 lines).
* From **Users**, **See activity** opens one person's history.
* Passwords and IP addresses are never shown.

## 23. Wiki: processes, guides and answers

In the menu, **Help & support → Wiki** (or at **app.sensumconstrucciones.com/wiki**).
It explains every process in the panel with real screenshots. It's only shown after
signing in, to every account.

* **Search:** type what you need to do ("change a photo", "export leads"); accents and
  capitals don't matter. The **/** key jumps to the search box.
* **For your role / The whole panel:** at first you see your sections' articles. "The
  whole panel" adds the others, marked "Not in your role", with **Ask for access**.
* **Screenshots:** shown in your language and appearance (light or dark). At the top of
  each article choose to see them as on a **Computer** or a **Phone**; tap one to
  enlarge it.
* **Ask support:** select any text in an article and tap **Ask support**. A request
  opens with the topic and the selected text; you only write your question. The "Still
  have a question?" card works too.
* **What's new:** when the panel adds something you can use, it shows you once, after
  signing in, what it is and what you can do, with **See how it works**. **Later**
  brings it back next time. In the menu, **Wiki** counts the updates you haven't seen;
  they're also at the top of the wiki's home page.

## 24. On phones and tablets

The panel is the same on any screen; on small ones it adapts like this:

* **Buttons for fingers:** on touch screens buttons are at least 44 px, including the
  ✕ of dialogs and the menu button.
* **Filters:** in Leads and Support the statuses are one row that slides sideways (the
  selected one always in view). In Leads (person in charge and service) and Activity
  (person, kind and dates) the other filters are under **Filters**; the button says how
  many you're using. That way the list starts on the first screen.
* **Photos & files:** the open tab is always visible. Under **Types of projects** the six
  projects start folded, each with its card photo and how many photos its gallery has;
  tap one to open it.
* **Users:** below 1100 px wide (tablets and small laptops) the list is one card per
  person; on wider screens, a table with every e-mail in full.
* **What's new:** the progress ("1 of 5") sits under the title, always in view.
