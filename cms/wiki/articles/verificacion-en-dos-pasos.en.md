Besides your password, the panel asks for a 6-digit code from an app on your phone that changes every 30 seconds. Even if someone knew your password, they couldn't sign in without your phone.

## Turning it on

1. Install **Google Authenticator**, **Microsoft Authenticator** or **1Password** on your phone.
2. In `My account` → **Two-step sign-in**, tap `Turn on two-step sign-in` and type your password.
3. In the app, add an account and **scan the QR code** (or type the key by hand).
4. Type the 6-digit code the app shows.
5. Keep the **10 recovery codes** somewhere safe. Each works once.

![Turning it on with the QR code](shot:dos-pasos)

## Signing in with it

After your password, type the code the app shows. Without your phone at hand, tap `Use a recovery code`.

> [!tip] If the owner account allows it, you can tick "Remember this device for 30 days" on the office computer. Never on a shared one.

## If you change or lose your phone

1. In `My account` → **Two-step sign-in**, tap `Remove registered devices` and type your password. All your sessions end.
2. When you sign in again, after your password **Check your e-mail** appears: type the 6-digit code we sent you. It expires in 10 minutes; if it doesn't arrive, tap `Resend code`.
3. **Register your new device** by scanning the QR code with the app on your new phone.

![Check your e-mail: the code to sign in](shot:codigo-correo)

> [!warn] Lost your phone and your recovery codes? Ask whoever manages users (or technical support) to **remove your registered devices**. You'll sign in with the same e-mail code.
