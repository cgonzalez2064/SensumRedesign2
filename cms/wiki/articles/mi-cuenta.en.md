`My account` (in the menu or the menu with your name) has everything personal.

![My account: profile, role and password](shot:mi-cuenta)

## Profile

- **Name**, **panel language** (also your e-mails') and **appearance**.
- **Your role**: your role's name and the sections it includes. From there you can `Ask for more access` or `See all sections`.

## Changing your password

1. Type your current password.
2. Type the new one and confirm it. Use at least 10 characters: a phrase of several words is easy to remember and hard to guess.
3. Tap `Change password`.

Your sessions on other devices end and you get a notice by e-mail. The panel refuses passwords that appear in known data leaks.

> [!tip] Signed in on someone else's computer? Tap `Sign out on other devices`.

## Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in screen tap `Forgot your password?`
2. Type your e-mail. You'll get "Reset your password" (check your spam folder too).
3. Open the link and choose a new password. It expires in 60 minutes and works once.

> [!important] Nobody from the technical team will ever ask for your password or your codes, by e-mail or by phone.

## Temporary password

If whoever manages users gave you a password to sign in, it expires after **72 hours**. Use it soon: when you sign in, the panel asks you to create your own before anything else.
