With **Support request management**, `Support` shows **everyone's** requests, not just yours.

![Request management with the support overview](shot:centro-de-ayuda)

## Handling a request

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| Mark in progress | Shows you're working on it. |
| Ask for more information | The person gets an e-mail and the request becomes "Waiting for information". |
| Mark as resolved | With a message of what you did. The person can rate the help. |
| Reply | A message for the person, with or without an e-mail notice. |
| Internal note | Only technical support sees it. |

The **support overview** (at the top, it folds away) shows over 30, 90 or 365 days the open requests, response time, share within the target and satisfaction.

> [!note] Questions from the wiki come with the article's topic and the text the person selected.
