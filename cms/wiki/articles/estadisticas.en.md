`Statistics` shows how many people visit the site, where they come from and how many get in touch, compared with the previous period.

![Statistics with the period, Export and Send by e-mail](shot:estadisticas)

## What each figure means

| Figure | What it counts |
|---|---|
| Visits | Different people who came to the site, counted per day. |
| Contact clicks | Taps on WhatsApp, phone and e-mail. |
| Form messages | Leads received (if you see Leads). |
| Contact rate | Clicks and messages per 100 visits. |

Further down: visits per day, where they come from (Google, Facebook, direct…), devices and language, and the path **from message to project** (contacted, quoted, won).

## Choosing the period

In **Period** choose the last 7, 30 or 90 days, **this month**, a full month or `Choose dates…` (up to a year). Below it, the period the figures are compared with. Under "From message to project" you can look at one **service**.

## Exporting

`Export` offers:

- `Spreadsheet (CSV)`: every figure for the period, for Excel or Google Sheets.
- `Print or save as PDF`: the screen, ready to print.

## Sending by e-mail

1. Tap `Send by e-mail`.
2. Type up to 5 addresses, separated by commas.
3. Tick the parts to include. The summary always goes.
4. If you like, write a message, choose the language and tick "Send me a copy".
5. Tap `Send report`. Each person gets their own e-mail, with the spreadsheet attached.

![Sending the report by e-mail](shot:estadisticas-correo)

> [!note] Visits are measured on the site itself, without cookies or personal data, and the report only has totals. That's why the figures are somewhat lower than other tools'.
