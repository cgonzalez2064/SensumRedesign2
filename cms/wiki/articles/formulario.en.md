In `Contact form` you decide what the site asks the visitor.

![Contact form: questions and preview](shot:formulario)

## Changing or adding questions

1. Tap a question's pencil to change it: the Spanish text (English is optional), a small help text, whether it's **Required** and whether it takes half a row or the full row. Tap `Done`.
2. With `Add a question` choose the kind of answer: short text, paragraph, e-mail, phone, number, date, drop-down list, single choice or checkboxes.
3. Order them by dragging the handle or with the arrows.
4. Check the **Preview**: it works like the real form (sends nothing) and switches between phone, tablet and computer, and between Spanish and English.
5. Tap `Save and publish`.

## Rules that don't change

- The **name** is always asked.
- **E-mail** and **phone** can be removed one at a time, but at least one stays and is required, so you can answer.
- Up to 15 questions and 20 options per question.

> [!warn] Ask only what you need: short forms get more messages. Don't ask for sensitive data such as ID documents, health or bank details.

Answers arrive in each message's e-mail and are kept in [Leads](wiki:prospectos).
