The **Content Manager** is where the Sensum Construcciones team updates the website, handles the contact-form messages and checks the results. It works in any browser, on a computer, tablet or phone.

## Signing in

1. Open **app.sensumconstrucciones.com** (bookmark it).
2. Type your e-mail and password and tap `Sign in`.
3. If you use two-step sign-in, type the 6-digit code from your app.

![The sign-in screen](shot:login)

> [!tip] The first time you sign in, the panel asks you to choose the language and appearance. You can change them any time in `My account`.

## What you see on Home

![Home with the menu, the top bar and the shortcuts](shot:inicio)

- **Side menu:** only shows your role's sections. On a phone, open it with the button at the top left.
- **Top bar:** `Request support`, `View website` and your name, which opens your menu (My account, language, appearance and sign out).
- **Centre:** the first steps for your role, shortcuts and the site's status. Once you tap `Got it`, the first steps don't show again on any device.

## Language and appearance

In the menu with your name choose **Español** or **English** (also the language of the e-mails we send you) and the appearance: **Light**, **Dark** or **System**, which follows your device. This wiki uses the same choices: its screenshots change with you.

## Signing out

Open the menu with your name and tap `Sign out`. Always do it on a shared computer.

> [!note] For security, your session ends by itself after 60 minutes without activity. If that happens while you're editing, the panel asks for your password right there and you don't lose your work.
