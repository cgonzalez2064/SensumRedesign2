## An error message

Read it: it almost always says what happened and how to go on. If it includes a **reference code**, note it and share it in your support request: it helps find the problem right away.

## The screen won't load

1. Check your internet connection. If it drops, the panel tells you at the top.
2. Reload the page.
3. If the panel says there's a new version, tap `Reload`.

## A change doesn't show on the site

See [When a change shows on the site](wiki:cambios-en-el-sitio).

## I can't sign in

- Forgot your password? Use `Forgot your password?` on the sign-in screen.
- Changed or lost your two-step sign-in phone? See [Two-step sign-in](wiki:verificacion-en-dos-pasos).
- After several failed attempts, the panel asks you to wait a few minutes: it's a protection.

> [!tip] If none of this works, tap `Request support` or write to it@gruposensum.com.
