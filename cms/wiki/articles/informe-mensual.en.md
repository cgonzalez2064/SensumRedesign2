`Monthly report` (under **Site status**) shows each month's report, with two tabs:

- **For clients:** what is sent by e-mail and as a PDF: availability, visits, potential clients, published changes, resolved requests, the month's highlights and next steps.
- **Technical detail:** internal, never sent: security, outages, errors, backups, certificate and domain, and technical to-dos.

![Monthly report: the month's preview](shot:informe)

Choose the month at the top; the last 13 are there.

> [!note] Your role can view the report. Sending it to clients is done by the owner account.
