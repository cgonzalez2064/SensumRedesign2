In `Users` you manage the team's accounts. If you're not the owner account, you only change people with the **same or less access** than yours; the others show a lock.

![Users: access requests, filters and each person with their role](shot:usuarios)

## Inviting someone

1. Tap `Invite user`.
2. Type their name and e-mail. If the e-mail looks mistyped ("gmial.com"), the panel suggests the right one.
3. Choose the **role**. For an exception, open **Extra sections** and add what the role doesn't include.
4. If it's for a while (agencies, photographers), turn on **Temporary access** and choose the date.
5. Tap `Send invitation`. They get a link to create their password, which expires in 72 hours.

![Inviting: the roles and, folded away, the extra sections](shot:invitar)

## Access requests

When someone asks for a section, it appears above the list with what they ask for and their message. `Grant access` applies it in one click and `Dismiss` closes it; either way we tell them by e-mail. On **Home**, the **Access** card reminds you what's waiting.

## Each person's ⋮ menu

| Option | What for |
|---|---|
| Update information | Correct their name or e-mail. A new e-mail asks for your password and tells the old and new address. |
| Send a new-password link | The safest option: they get a link that expires in 24 hours. |
| Set password | If e-mails don't reach them. The panel creates an easy-to-read one and gives you the message ready for WhatsApp. It expires in 72 hours. |
| Two-step sign-in | Require it, or remove their registered devices if they lost or changed their phone. |
| Close their sessions | For a lost phone or computer. |
| See activity | What they did and what changed in their account. |
| Change role | Another role or extra sections. It applies right away. |

> [!important] Passwords are never e-mailed: share it yourself by a safe means.

> [!tip] When someone leaves the company, turn their account off that same day with `Disable`.
