The menu only shows what you can open. **More sections** shows the rest of the panel, with what each part includes, so you can ask for what you need.

## Where it is

- At the end of the menu: `More sections`.
- On **Home**, in the card with the same name.
- In **My account**, with `See all sections`.
- If you open a section your role doesn't include, the panel says so and offers `Ask for access` or `See all sections`.

![Panel sections: what you can ask for and what you already have](shot:mas-secciones)

## Asking for a section

1. Under **You can ask for**, find the section you need and tap `Ask for access`.
2. The request opens with that section ticked. If you need more, tick them too.
3. Optionally, say what you need it for.
4. Tap `Send request`.

![The access request with the section already ticked](shot:pedir-acceso)

We e-mail whoever manages users. While they review it, the section shows as **Requested**; you get an e-mail when they answer. If it's approved, it appears in your menu when you reload the panel.

> [!tip] Changed your mind? Tap `Change request`: the new one replaces the previous one.

> [!note] "User management" and "Support request management" aren't asked for: the owner account gives them directly.
