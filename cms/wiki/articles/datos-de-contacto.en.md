In `Contact details` you change how clients reach you. Each detail updates the whole site at once: the contact section, the footer, the green WhatsApp button, the error page and the privacy notice.

![Contact details, showing how they'll look](shot:datos-de-contacto)

## Each detail

- **Mobile phone** is optional: if empty, the site shows only the office number.
- **WhatsApp:** with the country code, for example +502 3481 9804.
- **Address:** the panel shows how it will look in each place.
- **Google Maps:** in Google Maps search for the address, tap **Share** and copy the link. Check it with `Test link`.

Tap `Save changes` and it's published right away.

> [!tip] The questions the contact form asks are changed in [Contact form](wiki:formulario).
