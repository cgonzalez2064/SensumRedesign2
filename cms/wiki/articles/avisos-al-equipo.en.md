When the panel gets new features, each person sees them once when they sign in (the **What's new** items). With `Team announcements` you also tell them by e-mail, with a link to the wiki article that explains how they work.

![Team announcements: the updates, your message and who gets it](shot:avisos)

## Sending an announcement

1. Open `Team announcements` (**Administration** menu). After each update, Home reminds you if there are updates the team hasn't had by e-mail.
2. Tick the updates. The ones you haven't sent to everyone yet come ticked; each says who it's for and how many people already saw it in the panel or got it by e-mail.
3. If you want, write a **message**: it appears above the updates, with your name.
4. Check **Who gets it?**: each person with how many updates they get and in which language.
5. Tap `Send me a test` to see the e-mail in your inbox, then `Send announcement`.

> [!tip] Nobody gets the same update twice. If someone didn't get it (for example, because the e-mail failed), send again: it only goes to whoever is missing.

## Who gets it

- **Active** accounts that can use each update, with the same rule as the panel: for example, the Statistics one only goes to whoever has Statistics or Leads.
- The owner account, disabled accounts and whoever joined after the update (they got the welcome) don't get it.
- `Only whoever hasn't seen it in the panel` leaves out whoever already saw it when signing in.
- `Include whoever already got it by e-mail` sends it to everyone again.

Each send stays under **Earlier sends** and in `Activity`.

> [!important] It needs the panel's e-mail set up (**E-mail** menu). If it isn't, the page says so and doesn't let you send.
