In `Website text` you change what the website says. Each card is one part of the page and explains where it appears.

![Website text: each card is one part of the page](shot:textos)

## Changing a text

1. Open the section, for example **Home → Services**.
2. Each text has two versions: **Spanish** (the main one) and **English** (shown when a visitor picks "EN" on the site).
3. Type the new text. Below it you see how many characters you've used and the maximum: the limits keep the design right on phones and computers.
4. Tap `Save changes` in the bar at the bottom. The change is published right away, with a link to see it.

![A section's editor, with Spanish and English](shot:textos-editor)

## Automatic translation

If it's on, typing in Spanish fills the English by itself (and the other way round). Below it, "Translated automatically" appears: read it and correct it if needed before saving. `Undo` brings back the previous text.

> [!tip] To **highlight a word in colour** in the main title, type an asterisk before it and another after it. The panel shows you how it looks before saving.

## Good to know

- `Restore original text` brings back the site's approved text.
- The characters < and > can't be used.
- If you try to leave with unsaved changes, the panel warns you.
- If someone else saved the same section while you were editing, the panel warns you so nobody's work is lost.

## Title and description on Google

The **Google (search results)** section holds what Google and link previews (WhatsApp, Facebook) show. It isn't seen on the page. The title goes up to 60 characters and the description between 120 and 160. Google may take days or weeks to show the change.
