When you tap `Save changes` or `Publish`, the change is published on the site **right then**. Nothing else is needed.

## If you don't see it

1. Force-reload the site's page: **Ctrl + F5** on Windows or **Cmd + Shift + R** on Mac. On a phone, close the tab and open it again.
2. Wait a few seconds: large photos take a moment to process.
3. If it's still the same, check that you saved: the panel shows "Changes published on the site".
4. If it still doesn't show, [ask support](wiki:solicitar-soporte) saying what you changed and where.

> [!note] People with **Publishing and activity** see on Home whether the pages are up to date and can `Republish the site` if technical support asks them to.
