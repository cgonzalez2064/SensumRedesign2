`Photos & files` has three tabs: **About**, **Types of projects** and **Documents**. Each spot explains where its photo appears on the site.

On a phone, the six projects under **Types of projects** start folded, each with its card photo and how many photos its gallery has: tap one to open it.

![Photos & files: the "About" photo with its description](shot:fotos)

## Uploading or changing a photo

1. Tap `Upload photo` or `Change image` (in a gallery, `Add photo`).
2. Choose the image. On a phone you can use the gallery or the camera.
3. Check the **preview**: it shows how it will be cropped on the site. If needed, choose the **Framing** (Top, Centre, Bottom).
4. Write a short **description** of what it shows, for example "Kitchen remodelled with wooden cabinets". It helps people with visual impairments and Google.
5. Tap `Publish`. You'll see the upload progress.

> [!tip] There's no need to edit photos first: the system crops, resizes and optimises them by itself.

## Which photos work best

| Where | Shape | Recommended size |
|---|---|---|
| Projects (card and gallery) | Landscape, 4:3 | 1600 × 1200 or more |
| About | Square or portrait, the important part in the centre | 1600 × 1600 or more |

JPG, PNG and WebP, up to 10 MB. Use real, sharp, well-lit photos the company has permission to publish.

## Other actions

- **Edit description** of a published photo.
- **Remove photo**: the original illustration comes back.
- In galleries, the arrows change the order.

## PDF portfolio

In the **Documents** tab, `Replace PDF` uploads the portfolio downloaded from the site. Up to 20 MB.
