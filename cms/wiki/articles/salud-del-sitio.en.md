`Site health` (under **Site status**) tells you at a glance whether the site is working.

![Site health: notice, availability and charts](shot:salud)

## How to read it

- **The notice at the top:** "Everything works", "There was a problem" in the last 24 hours or "The site has a problem right now". Technical support gets the alert automatically.
- **Availability:** the share of time the site was online in 24 hours, 7, 30 and 90 days.
- **Response time:** how fast it loads, on average.
- **Incidents (30 days):** when there were failures and how long they lasted.
- **Security and backups:** site published and up to date, certificate and domain valid, domain e-mail and the latest backup.

> [!tip] It's a view-only screen. If you notice something odd on the site, tell support with `Request support`.
