Each person has a **role**, which decides which sections they see in the menu and what they can change. Your role is shown in `My account`.

## The four roles

| Role | What it can use |
|---|---|
| **Administrator** | The whole panel: text, photos, contact details and form, publishing and activity, Leads, Statistics, Site health, Monthly report (view) and user management. |
| **Website editor** | Text, photos, PDF portfolio, contact details and form. |
| **Sales and leads** | Contact-form messages with their follow-up, and visit statistics. Cannot change the site. |
| **View only** | Visit statistics, site health and the monthly report. Cannot change anything. |

## Extra sections

For a one-off case, whoever manages users can add a section a role doesn't include: for example, **Website editor + Leads**. They are also added when an access request of yours is approved.

## Need another section?

No need to call or write: in [More sections](wiki:mas-secciones) you see what you can ask for and ask in one click.

> [!note] The **owner account** (technical support) can use everything, including technical settings. Only it gives "User management" and "Support request management".
