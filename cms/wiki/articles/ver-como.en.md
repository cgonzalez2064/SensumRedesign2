`View as…` (in Users) shows the panel **exactly as another person sees it**: their menu, screens and data. It's for checking their role or walking them through something.

1. In `Users`, tap `View as…` or the person's ⋮ menu.
2. Choose the person and confirm with your password.
3. A blue bar at the top shows their name, their role, "read-only" and the time left.
4. Tap `Back to my account` when you're done.

> [!note] It's read-only: nothing is saved, and it ends by itself after 30 minutes.
