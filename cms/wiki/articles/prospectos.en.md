Every message sent through the site's form is kept in `Leads`, even if the notice e-mail fails. The menu shows how many nobody has opened yet.

![Leads: the list with filters and who's in charge of each message](shot:prospectos)

## Following up a message

1. Open the message. From there you can **reply by e-mail**, **write on WhatsApp** or **call**.
2. Change the **status**: New, Contacted, Quoted, Won or Lost.
3. Note the **amount** quoted or won, in quetzales.
4. Add follow-up **notes** ("Called on Tuesday, sending the quote on Friday").

Every change is kept in the history with who and when.

![A message with its status, person in charge and notes](shot:prospecto)

## Person in charge and automation

- **Person in charge:** whoever follows it up. If you choose someone else, we tell them by e-mail. In the list, filter by **Assigned to me** or **Unassigned**.
- **Automation** (below the list): who handles each service. Each new message is assigned by itself.
- **Reminder:** if a message is still "New" after 24, 48 or 72 hours, we write to the person in charge once.
- **Weekly summary:** on Mondays, how many arrived and how many are waiting.

## Searching and exporting

- Filters by status and service; search by name, e-mail, phone, text or number (#12).
- `Export CSV` downloads the filtered list for Excel or Google Sheets.

> [!important] Leads holds clients' **personal data**. Don't share it outside the company. If someone asks us to delete their data, use `Delete` on their message.
