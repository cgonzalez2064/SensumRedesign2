`Request support` is at the top of every screen. Use it for a problem, an improvement, a change you can't make or a question.

![Asking for support: type, title and description](shot:soporte)

## Sending a request

1. Choose the type: problem, improvement, content, design or other.
2. Write a short title and describe what happened or what you need.
3. Say whether it's on the website, in the panel or both.
4. If you can, add a screenshot.
5. Tap `Send request`.

Each request gets a number and stays in `Support` (Help Center), where you see its status and the conversation. We tell you by e-mail when there's an answer.

![Support: your requests and their status](shot:centro-de-ayuda)

## Asking from the wiki

Have a question about something you read here? **Select the text** in the article and tap `Ask support`: the request opens with the topic and the text you selected, and you only write your question. You can also use the "Still have a question?" card.

> [!important] Don't include passwords or sensitive data in a request. The technical team will never ask for them.

If you can't sign in to the panel, write to it@gruposensum.com.
